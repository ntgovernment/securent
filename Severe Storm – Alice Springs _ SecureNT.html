Severe Storm – Alice Springs

Latest update

What: Tropical Cyclone Fina is a category 3 system approaching [location].

When: expected to cross the NT coast at [location] within 12 hours.

What you need to do: check the warning level at your location below and follow the advice.

Stay indoors and avoid unnecessary travel while severe weather conditions continue. Never drive, walk or ride through floodwaters.

Read more

Last updated: 25 November 2026 at 3.47pm

An emergency warning/watch and act/advice is now active for these locations: Darwin Palmerston Northern Rivers and North West Slope Katherine Tennant Creek Nhulunbuy Jabiru Yulara Batchelor

ALERT LEVEL: Emergency. An Emergency Warning is the highest level of Bushfires Alert. You may be in danger and need to take action immediately. Any delay now puts your life at risk.

INFORMATION CURRENT AS AT: 23/08/2026 17:10:00

STATUS: GOING - A fire which is spreading on one or more fronts. Effective containment strategies are not in place for the entire perimeter.

RISKS FACED BY PEOPLE IN THE AREA: Smoke from this fire may affect visibility, Active fire may occur close to the roadside , Firefighting crews may be working close to the roadside , The risk of loss of life or threat to properties is almost certain or has occurred , Bushfires NT is primarily undertaking defensive strategies to protect lives and property.

WHAT TO DO: If unsafe to stay, leave immediately. If unsafe to leave, shelter indoors immediately. Conditions will change, continue to monitor conditions.

More information

The following alert has been issued by the Northern Territory Government

Please note warning area has changed to Bidyadanga to Carnarvon and extending inland including Barrow and Monte Bello Islands. Please note changes to Cyclone details.

Issued: 25 Mar 2026 09:25 AM

As at 8:56am on Wednesday 25 March 2026, the Bureau of Meteorology advised Tropical Cyclone Narelle has redeveloped into a Category 1 tropical cyclone overnight and is expected to continue to intensify to reach severe (category 3) intensity overnight tonight.

A Cyclone Advice is in place for CAPE LEVEQUE to south of BEAGLE BAY.

Although there is no immediate danger you need to start preparing for dangerous weather and keep up to date.

WHAT TO DO

  • Review your plan and home maintenance, and make sure everyone in your household knows what to do. If you do not have a plan, make one now on Emergency WA or in the Emergency WA app.
  • Check your emergency kit has key items like a first aid kit and an AM/FM radio that runs off batteries.
  • Know the cyclone warning system and the steps that must be taken for each level.
  • Monitor the Emergency WA website.
  • Remember that preparing your home and property is your responsibility.
  • For more information on how to prepare visit emergency.wa.gov.au/prepare.

More information

Our emergency hotline 1800 888 901 is operational 7am to 7pm daily. This is for people looking for information about shelters, roads and transport and general information about the cyclone response.

Call 1800 245 090 to report any fallen powerlines.

For emergency service in floods, storms or cyclones call 132 500.

DO NOT CALL 000 unless it is an emergency.
